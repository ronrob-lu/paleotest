# 🦖 PaleoTest: Prehistoric Rebirth

> **Bring the Ancient World to Life in Luanti!**

---

> ⚠️ **Notice**: This mod is a fork, revamped with AI for modern Luanti by **ronrob-lu 2026**.

---

Welcome to **PaleoTest**, the ultimate prehistoric expansion mod for **Luanti** (formerly Minetest)! Step back millions of years into an era dominated by apex predators, majestic giants, soaring pterosaurs, and ancient ocean leviathans.

Whether you are building a modern Jurassic zoo, excavating ancient fossils in deep caverns, or taming a Tyrannosaurus to dominate the wild, **PaleoTest** delivers a deep, immersive, and thrilling prehistoric experience!

---

## ✨ Features at a Glance

### 🦖 19 Prehistoric Species
Discover a diverse cast of land, air, and aquatic creatures, each with unique models, behaviors, and soundscapes:

- **Apex Predators & Land Giants**: *Tyrannosaurus rex, Velociraptor, Spinosaurus, Brachiosaurus, Stegosaurus, Triceratops, Carnotaurus*
- **Rulers of the Skies**: *Pteranodon, Quetzalcoatlus*
- **Ocean Terrors**: *Mosasaurus, Plesiosaurus, Dunkleosteus, Sarcosuchus*
- **Cenozoic Beasts**: *Mammoth, Smilodon, Dire Wolf, Elasmotherium, Procoptodon, Thylacoleo*

---

### 🔬 Science & DNA Cultivation Loop
Do not just hunt dinosaurs—**resurrect them**!

1. ⛏️ **Unearth Fossils**: Dig deep underground to discover rare bio-fossils and ancient frozen remains.
2. 🧪 **Analyze Samples**: Process raw fossils in the **Fossil Analyzer** to extract pure genetic DNA sequences.
3. 🧬 **Cultivate & Incubate**: Synthesize DNA with the **DNA Cultivator** to synthesize eggs and embryonic syringe samples.
4. 🐣 **Hatch & Raise**: Care for baby dinos, feed them with specialized **Herbivore & Carnivore Feeders**, and tame them as loyal companions or mounts!

---

### 🎮 Universal Compatibility
Engineered with modular abstraction, **PaleoTest** runs out-of-the-box on multiple Luanti game engines without hassle:
- 🌿 **Minetest Game** (`default`, `stairs`, `walls`)
- 📦 **MineClone / VoxeLibre** (`mcl_*` suite)
- 🛡️ Built-in fallback stubs for core mob libraries (`mobkit` & `mob_core`) ensuring zero crashes!

## ⚙️ Configuration & Options

Fine-tune your prehistoric world using Luanti's setting configuration (`settingtypes.txt` / World Settings):

| Setting | Type | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `breeding` | Boolean | `true` | Enables/disables creature breeding and reproduction mechanics. |
| `block_breaking` | Boolean | `false` | Toggles whether giant apex predators can break weak terrain blocks. |
| `legacy_convert` | Boolean | `true` | Seamlessly converts items and world nodes from older PaleoTest 1.x worlds. |

---

## 🛠️ Installation

1. Download or clone this repository into your Luanti `mods/` directory:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/ronrob-lu/paleotest.mod.git paleotest
   ```
2. Enable `paleotest` in your Luanti world settings.
3. Jump in and start your prehistoric adventure!
